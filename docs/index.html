How each metric is calculated

All data comes from ESPN's league endpoint, one request per season:

https://lm-api-reads.fantasy.espn.com/apis/v3/games/ffl/seasons/{year}/segments/0/leagues/{leagueId}?view=mTeam&view=mMatchup&view=mSettings&view=mDraftDetail&view=mRoster

Seasons before 2018 use /leagueHistory/{leagueId}?seasonId={year}. Private leagues need the espn_s2 and SWID cookies sent with each request.

Trade analyzer

This season's completed trades, from your league's Recent Activity feed. Each player's positional rank by points scored before the trade vs. from the trade week on. A trade is won by the side whose new players scored more points in its starting lineup. Ranks and points use your league's scoring. ESPN doesn't keep trade history for past seasons.
