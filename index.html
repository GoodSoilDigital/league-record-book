How each metric is calculated

All data comes from ESPN's league endpoint, one request per season:

https://lm-api-reads.fantasy.espn.com/apis/v3/games/ffl/seasons/{year}/segments/0/leagues/{leagueId}?view=mTeam&view=mMatchup&view=mSettings&view=mDraftDetail&view=mRoster

Seasons before 2018 use /leagueHistory/{leagueId}?seasonId={year}. Private leagues need the espn_s2 and SWID cookies sent with each request.

Draft report card

First-round busts are first-round picks who finished outside the top 20 at their position. Late gems are picks from round 7 on who finished top 20 at RB or WR, or top 8 at QB or TE; kickers and defenses are left out. Trade analyzer

Every completed trade from 2019 on, read from ESPN's weekly rosters (ESPN has no lineup history before 2019). Each side gets a grade from points per week vs. no trade: every regular-season week after the deal, we take the lineup the team actually set, pull out the players it received, and fill those spots with the best of what it traded away or had on the bench. The grade moves up or down a step for each half win the trade added or cost in all-play wins (how many of the league's lineups the team would have beaten each week), which measures value without schedule luck. A player's points only count for the team he was on. Trades need 3 weeks of games before they're graded. Player ranks show where each player stood at the trade, for the rest of the season, and at season's end, so you can spot who bought low and who sold high.
